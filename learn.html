Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The daily quiz, stars, streaks and stickers are free forever. Premium is optional and adds unlimited practice in every subject, more owl chats and progress insights.

Is it safe for my child?

There are no ads and no chat with other people. Children never create accounts: you, the parent, do. A maths question protects the grown-ups area, so children can't change settings or pay.

What ages is it for?

Four levels: 2–3 (Little Explorers), 4–5 (Nursery), 6–8 (Primary 1–3) and 9–11 (Primary 4–6). Questions match the age you choose.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. A Google Play app is planned.

What information do you collect?

Your name and email, and for each child just a first name or nickname, age and a cartoon avatar. No photos, no location. Read the full Privacy Policy. You can delete your account any time.

Who is behind it?

Cedarville Private Schools in Aguda, Surulere, Lagos, serving families since 2014. Visit our school website.
