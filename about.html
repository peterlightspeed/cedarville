About Cedarville Schools

Dedicated to nurturing young minds since 2014

Our Mission

To nurture leaders by providing world-class quality education in a safe, comfortable, and stimulating environment.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational institution that is renowned, both locally and globally, for excellence.

Our Story

A Decade of Nurturing Young Minds

Cedarville Private School opened in 2014. Its founding owners had a vision to groom leaders into being the very best version of themselves. In June 2022, the school was taken over by a new board in order to meet the growing demand for exceptional service.

The new owners share a vision of building a system that grooms excellent leaders who will impact society at large. By September 2022, a stronger structure with modern amenities had been put in place across the school.

The curriculum was then aligned with both British and Nigerian standards, in line with our vision to be a school renowned for excellence not just locally but globally.

At Cedarville, we care for children from as young as 6 months old to 11 years. We are a Cambridge International Examinations accredited school, and our staff are highly qualified and experienced in taking care of our children's learning needs.
