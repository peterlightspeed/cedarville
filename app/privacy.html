Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for school review. This policy describes how the app is built to work. Before publishing, please have it checked by a qualified adviser for the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and Google Play's Families Policy, and fill in the school's registered details below.

Who we are

Cedarville Learning is provided by Cedarville Private Schools, Aguda, Surulere, Lagos, Nigeria ("we"). Contact: cedarvilleprivate@gmail.com, +234 813 745 1764.

Parents create the account — children do not

Only a parent or guardian (18 or over) can create an account. Children use profiles that the parent sets up. Children never create their own login, and the app does not let children contact other users or share anything publicly.

What we collect

  • Parent: name, email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Child profile: a first name or nickname, age, and a cartoon avatar. We ask parents not to use a full name.
  • Learning activity: quiz answers, scores, stars and streaks, so we can show progress.
  • Where you came from: if you arrive from an advert or a shared link, we remember a short tag such as "facebook" or "parent_share" with your account, so we can learn which adverts actually help families. Before you sign up we also count anonymous visits and free-quiz plays (no IP address, no cookies, no name).
  • Classes and homework (only if you choose to join a class): when a parent enters a teacher's class code, that class's teacher can see the child's first name, avatar, age and results for that class's homework. Children in the same class can see first names, avatars and star counts on the class leaderboard; a parent can hide their child from the leaderboard, or take their child out of the class, at any time in the Grown-ups area. There is no school-wide or public leaderboard.
  • Teachers: a teacher's name and email, so the school can approve teacher access. Teachers never see parents' contact details.
  • Premium codes: if you redeem a code from the school, we record which code was used and when.
  • Reading aloud: questions are read by your own device's built-in voice. Nothing you hear is sent to us.
  • Payments: handled by Paystack. We receive a payment reference and status, never card details.
  • Cedar the Owl (only when switched on): Cedar is an AI, a computer program, not a person, and the app tells children and parents so. A child's typed question is sent to our AI provider (currently Anthropic) to produce a reply. We send only the question text and an age band, never the child's name, email or account details, and we remove phone numbers and email addresses first. A safety filter stops unsuitable questions before they reach the AI, replies are checked so they carry no links or contact details, and any reply can be reported with the 🚩 button. School staff review reports. Messages are not used for advertising.

What we do not do

  • No advertising inside the app, and no third-party ad trackers anywhere in the children's area. We never sell data.
  • No photos, voice recordings, precise location or contacts are collected.
  • No chat between children or with strangers.

Videos

The Watch & Learn section is switched off for now. If we switch it on, only videos that the school owns or has permission to show will be used, played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may process data under its own policy when a video is played.

Where data is stored and for how long

Data is stored with our database and hosting providers (Neon, Render and Vercel). We keep it while the account is active. A parent can delete any child profile (and its progress) from the Grown-ups area at any time, and can ask us to delete the whole account by email.

Children's privacy

We design the app around the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and general children's-privacy principles: a parent or guardian gives consent and holds the account, we collect as little as possible about children, we show no advertising to them, and parents can delete their child's data at any time.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your family's data by contacting us at the address above. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents in the app.
